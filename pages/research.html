Dating Research

A location-aware relationship research application shaped by the importance of accessible, comfortable places to meet.

Explore the application →
How the work connects: The aiChair work established a long-running focus on assistive technology. ACCESS Reports brought lived experience of accessibility into public view. The Access Report application extended that work into structured, location-based assessments. Dating Research grew from a related insight: the places where people meet can affect whether social connection is accessible and comfortable.
